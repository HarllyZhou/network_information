## Concepts

- [Credit spread](concepts/credit-spread.md) — The excess of a risky debt yield over the risk-free rate. The formula depends on how default is modelled. (updated 2026-10-16, 2 sources)
- [Diagnostic expectation](concepts/diagnostic-expectation.md) — A forecast that overweights outcomes recent news makes representative. One parameter governs the extra weight. (updated 2026-10-16, 2 sources)
- [Monetary policy](concepts/monetary-policy.md) — The monetary authority's nominal instrument. Papers write it as a policy rate or as a rule for nominal demand. (updated 2026-10-16, 3 sources)
- [Neutral rate](concepts/neutral-rate.md) — The real policy rate consistent with output at potential and stable inflation; unobserved and estimated with wide uncertainty. (updated 2026-09-21, 1 sources)
- [Production network](concepts/production-network.md) — Which industries use which goods as inputs. Those sets may be fixed, or each industry may choose them. (updated 2026-10-16, 3 sources)
