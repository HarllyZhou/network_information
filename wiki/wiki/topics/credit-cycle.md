# Credit cycle

## Methodology

- Bordalo, Gennaioli, Shleifer, and Terry link IBES manager guidance to Compustat and to FISD–TRACE bond data, then estimate a heterogeneous-firm model by simulated method of moments. Firms and lenders share one [diagnostic expectation](../concepts/diagnostic-expectation.md). The friction is one-period defaultable debt. The comparison model is the same economy with diagnosticity set to zero. Source: [Real Credit Cycles](../sources/2026-10-16-bordalo-et-al-real-credit-cycle.md).

## Results

- Fragility after good times. Good news raises investment and debt and compresses the [credit spread](../concepts/credit-spread.md), because firms and lenders both overreact. When the news ages, spreads rise, investment falls, and defaults rise, even if the fundamental shock is moderate. Diagnosticity near 1 matches the 2007–2009 spread spike. Rational expectations do not. Source: [Real Credit Cycles](../sources/2026-10-16-bordalo-et-al-real-credit-cycle.md).

## Problems and mitigations

- The baseline holds the wage and the risk-free rate fixed, the recovery rate is an exogenous fraction of depreciated capital, and debt lasts one period. The paper endogenizes the wage in general equilibrium and the reversal remains. No later source in the wiki yet addresses one-period debt or the absence of an intermediary. Source: [Real Credit Cycles](../sources/2026-10-16-bordalo-et-al-real-credit-cycle.md).
