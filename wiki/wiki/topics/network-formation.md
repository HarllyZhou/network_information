# Network formation

Papers that take the [production network](../concepts/production-network.md) as given do not belong here.

## How the network is formed

- Acemoglu and Azar. Each contestable industry chooses a supplier set \(S_i\) to minimize unit cost. Productivity \(A_i(S_i)\) depends on that set. The price is \(P_i^\ast = (1+\mu_i)K_i(S_i^\ast, A_i(S_i^\ast), P^\ast)\), so taxes, regulations, contracting frictions, or markups scale every price. Adoption of a set is costless, and any combination is admissible in the baseline. An equilibrium exists. Prices are unique, and the network is generically unique. Source: [Endogenous Production Networks](../sources/2020-01-01-acemoglu-azar-endogenous-production-networks.md).

## Consequences relative to a given network

- A better technology, or a lower distortion, weakly reduces every price. When the technology–price single-crossing condition holds, it also weakly expands every supplier set. Supermodular technologies, Hicks-neutral Cobb–Douglas, and CES with input-specific productivities satisfy the condition. Source: [Endogenous Production Networks](../sources/2020-01-01-acemoglu-azar-endogenous-production-networks.md).
- Equal distortions that are fully rebated leave the allocation efficient only when the efficient network has no links. Above a threshold they make the equilibrium network empty and lower welfare, because they distort the choice between labor and intermediates. Source: [Endogenous Production Networks](../sources/2020-01-01-acemoglu-azar-endogenous-production-networks.md).
- A small productivity change can jump GDP and the network. In a 391-sector calibration to the 2007 U.S. input–output table, a 1 percent TFP increase in computer and electronic product manufacturing adds 288 edges and raises real GDP by 0.72 percent, of which 0.59 percentage points come from other sectors. The same shock with no new links raises GDP by 0.04 percent under Cobb–Douglas, 0.01 percent under CES with \(\sigma = 2\), and 0.09 percent under CES with \(\sigma = 1/2\). Hulten's theorem gives about 0.03 percent. When the network is held fixed, GDP does not jump. Source: [Endogenous Production Networks](../sources/2020-01-01-acemoglu-azar-endogenous-production-networks.md).
- With Gumbel draws, normalized indegrees converge to zero and outdegrees stay dispersed. If the exogenous shares \(\alpha_{ij}\) have a power-law tail, outdegrees do too. Source: [Endogenous Production Networks](../sources/2020-01-01-acemoglu-azar-endogenous-production-networks.md).

## Problems and mitigations

- The technology menu is prespecified, adoption is costless, each industry is contestable, and labor is essential, with \(A_i(\emptyset) > 0\). The single-crossing condition is an extra restriction and fails for some cost functions. The cross-section uses Gumbel draws and a uniform bound on intermediate shares. Source: [Endogenous Production Networks](../sources/2020-01-01-acemoglu-azar-endogenous-production-networks.md).
- No later source in the wiki yet relaxes costless adoption or derives the technology menu from an invention decision.
