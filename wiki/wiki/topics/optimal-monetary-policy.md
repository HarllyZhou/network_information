# Optimal monetary policy

## Tradeoffs faced by policymakers

- La'O and Tahbaz-Salehi. Nominal rigidities create three losses: within-industry price dispersion, an output gap, and misallocation across industries from relative prices that differ from the flexible-price benchmark. One [monetary policy](../concepts/monetary-policy.md) instrument cannot remove all three. A flexible-price allocation is implementable only when some nominal-wage rule makes every firm's nominal marginal cost measurable with respect to its own information. With more than one sticky industry, that condition fails for a generic information structure, so the first best is not implementable. The tradeoff comes from the [production network](../concepts/production-network.md) and from heterogeneous stickiness. Steady-state markups are removed by non-contingent subsidies, so the tradeoff is not a response to markup shocks. If only one industry has sticky prices, stabilizing that industry's price implements any flexible-price allocation. Source: [Optimal Monetary Policy in Production Networks](../sources/2022-05-01-lao-tahbaz-salehi-optimal-monetary-policy-production-networks.md).
- Fang, Huo, Li, and Wu. Price flexibility is chosen by firms. The authority's wage rule changes the variance of marginal cost, so it changes how much firms learn. A rule that places more weight on sectors that are already rigid further reduces their incentive to acquire information and widens the dispersion of rigidities. Source: [Inattentive Network](../sources/2025-06-16-fang-huo-li-wu-inattentive-network.md).
- Carvalho, Eusepi, Moench, and Preston. A larger weight \(\lambda_x\) on the output gap makes [inflation expectation anchoring](../concepts/inflation-expectation-anchoring.md) more self-referential: a higher perceived inflation mean raises actual inflation, which confirms the belief. Strict inflation targeting removes that feedback. The true target in the model stays at zero, so the Great Inflation is a sequence of persistent forecast errors that switches firms onto the constant gain. Source: [Anchored Inflation Expectations](../sources/2023-01-01-carvalho-eusepi-moench-preston-anchored-inflation-expectations.md).

## Policy target

- The optimal policy stabilizes \(\sum_i \psi_i^\ast \log p_i = 0\). The weight on industry \(i\) is \(\psi_i^\ast = \psi_i^{\mathrm{og}} + \psi_i^{\mathrm{within}} + \psi_i^{\mathrm{across}}\), corresponding to the output gap, within-industry dispersion, and across-industry misallocation. The weight is larger when the industry has a larger Domar weight, is stickier, and sits further upstream, and when its suppliers are less sticky and its customers are stickier. Source: [Optimal Monetary Policy in Production Networks](../sources/2022-05-01-lao-tahbaz-salehi-optimal-monetary-policy-production-networks.md).
- In a 66-industry calibration, this target leaves a welfare loss of 0.65 percent of quarterly consumption relative to flexible prices. Of the quadratic approximation, 0.53 percentage points are within-industry misallocation and 0.14 are across-industry misallocation. Output-gap stabilization loses 0.67 percent, and the two weight vectors have cosine similarity 0.99. A consumption-share index loses 1.85 percent, a Domar-weight index 1.59 percent, and a stickiness-adjusted consumption index that ignores input–output linkages 1.46 percent. Both the optimal index and the output-gap index place the largest weight on the nominal wage. Source: [Optimal Monetary Policy in Production Networks](../sources/2022-05-01-lao-tahbaz-salehi-optimal-monetary-policy-production-networks.md).
- Carvalho, Eusepi, Moench, and Preston. The target criterion is \(\pi_t - \gamma\pi_{t-1} + \lambda_x x_t = \phi_t\). It is the optimal policy under discretion for a zero inflation target. The estimated \(\Gamma\) is about 0.89, so long-run beliefs remain close to a unit root. Source: [Anchored Inflation Expectations](../sources/2023-01-01-carvalho-eusepi-moench-preston-anchored-inflation-expectations.md).
- Fang, Huo, Li, and Wu. The target is \(\sum_i \varphi_i p_{it} = 0\) with \(\varphi_i = \varphi_i^x + \varphi_i^e\). The first weight holds flexibility fixed and coincides with the La'O and Tahbaz-Salehi index, including the case of a fixed information capacity. The second weight internalizes the response of flexibility to the wage rule. In the calibration the two weight vectors differ by 24.3 percent on average, and differences in initial flexibility account for more than 85 percent of that gap. Source: [Inattentive Network](../sources/2025-06-16-fang-huo-li-wu-inattentive-network.md).

## Problems and mitigations

- The model is static, so \(\gamma\) is a wealth effect on labor supply rather than an intertemporal elasticity. The closed-form target uses Cobb–Douglas technologies and a first-order approximation. Firms' signals do not depend on prices. The authority is assumed able to condition nominal demand on the full state, including beliefs. When the authority is itself information-constrained, the same weights remain optimal and the policy minimizes the volatility of that index. In the calibration, identical within-industry elasticities make the output-gap and within-industry weights line up; that alignment is a feature of the calibration. Source: [Optimal Monetary Policy in Production Networks](../sources/2022-05-01-lao-tahbaz-salehi-optimal-monetary-policy-production-networks.md).
- Fang, Huo, Li, and Wu replace the exogenous information structure with elastic attention. The economy remains static, the network remains given, and the closed form is still a price index. Source: [Inattentive Network](../sources/2025-06-16-fang-huo-li-wu-inattentive-network.md).
- Carvalho, Eusepi, Moench, and Preston do not use interest rates or output in the likelihood. The baseline switching rule is a distance from the true conditional expectation, which firms are assumed not to know. A calibrated recursive specification test produces a similar path for the gain. A gain that moves continuously does not reproduce the discrete shifts in long-run expectations. Source: [Anchored Inflation Expectations](../sources/2023-01-01-carvalho-eusepi-moench-preston-anchored-inflation-expectations.md).
