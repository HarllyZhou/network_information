# Credit spread

## Definition

The credit spread is the extra yield on a risky debt claim over the risk-free rate. In Bordalo, Gennaioli, Shleifer, and Terry the claim is one-period defaultable debt with price \(q\), and

\[S = \frac{1}{q} - R.\]

## Evidence

- Lenders who break even set \(q_\theta(s,k',b') = \frac{1}{R}\mathbb{E}^\theta[1 + d^f_\theta(s',k',b')(\ell(k',b') - 1) \mid s]\). The spread is high when diagnostic beliefs put more weight on default. Recovery \(\ell\) is a fraction \(\gamma\) of depreciated capital. Source: [Real Credit Cycles](../sources/2026-10-16-bordalo-et-al-real-credit-cycle.md).
