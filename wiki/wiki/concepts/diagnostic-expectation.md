# Diagnostic expectation

## Definition

A diagnostic expectation overweights future outcomes that recent news has made representative. For log TFP, and when agents overreact only to current news,

\[\mathbb{E}_t^\theta(\ln A_{t+1}) = \rho \ln A_t + \theta\rho\varepsilon_t.\]

\(\theta = 0\) is rational expectations. \(\theta > 0\) adds overreaction to the current shock, and that overreaction fades as the news ages, without requiring a bad shock.

## Evidence

- Bordalo, Gennaioli, Shleifer, and Terry estimate \(\theta\) near 1 from firm-level profit forecasts, and use that same value for firms and for lenders. Source: [Real Credit Cycles](../sources/2026-10-16-bordalo-et-al-real-credit-cycle.md).
